Data Processing Agreement

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Purpose and Scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the service agreement between the client ("Client" or "Controller"), operating under the name AetherLabs ("AetherLabs" or "Processor").

This DPA governs how AetherLabs processes personal data on behalf of the Client when providing AI voice agent services, call handling, appointment booking, integrations, and related services.

This DPA applies whenever AetherLabs processes personal data on the Client's behalf.

The parties agree to comply with their respective obligations under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), and applicable United States state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended), where applicable.

2. Roles of the Parties

For personal data processed through the services:

• The Client acts as the Data Controller (or "business") and determines the purposes and means of processing.
• AetherLabs acts as the Data Processor (or "service provider") and processes personal data on the Client's behalf according to documented instructions.

AetherLabs may separately act as an independent Data Controller when processing information for its own business operations, including account administration, billing, and business communications.

3. Categories of Personal Data

Depending on the services configured by the Client, AetherLabs may process:

• Callers' names and phone numbers.
• Email addresses.
• Call recordings and transcripts.
• Appointment and booking details.
• Information voluntarily provided by callers during conversations.
• Call metadata, including timestamps and call duration.

Categories of data subjects: callers who contact the Client's AI voice agents, individuals the Client's agents call, and the Client's staff and authorized users.

The services are not intended to process special categories of personal data or sensitive personal information (including health information, government ID numbers, and payment card numbers) unless specifically agreed upon in writing and legally permitted. AetherLabs does not process protected health information (PHI) unless the parties have signed a business associate agreement. AetherLabs does not create voiceprints or use voice recordings to identify or verify individuals.

4. Purposes of Processing

AetherLabs processes personal data to:

1. Answer and handle inbound calls.
2. Make outbound calls when configured by the Client.
3. Qualify leads and respond to inquiries.
4. Schedule, modify, and manage appointments.
5. Transfer calls to human representatives.
6. Generate call summaries, transcripts, and reports.
7. Provide dashboard access and service analytics.
8. Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the services and improve their operation, without using Client or caller data to train AI models (see Section 5).

5. Processing Instructions

AetherLabs agrees to:

• Process personal data only to provide the agreed services and follow the Client's documented instructions.
• Not use Client or caller data for advertising, marketing, or AI model training without separate written permission.
• Not sell or share personal data, and not retain, use, or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the Client or for any purpose other than the business purposes in Section 4.
• Not combine personal data received from or on behalf of the Client with personal data from other sources, except as permitted by applicable law.
• Notify the Client if AetherLabs determines it can no longer meet its obligations under applicable privacy laws. The Client may take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use of personal data.
• Process personal data when required by applicable law, where legally permitted to do so.
• Inform the Client if an instruction appears to violate applicable data protection laws, where legally permitted.

6. Security Measures

AetherLabs implements reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including:

• Encryption: Data encryption provided through its infrastructure providers, including Retell AI and Twilio.
• Access Restrictions: Access to Client information is limited to authorized AetherLabs personnel.
• Account Security: Two-factor authentication (2FA) is used on AetherLabs accounts.
• Data Deletion: Clients may request deletion of their personal data.
• Secure Infrastructure: Use of third-party providers with documented security practices.
• Confidentiality: Personnel authorized to access personal data are required to maintain its confidentiality.

These measures may be updated as AetherLabs' services and infrastructure develop. Further detail is available on our Security page.

7. Subprocessors

The Client authorizes AetherLabs to use the following third-party providers where necessary to deliver the services:

• Retell AI: Provides AI voice agent infrastructure, call processing, and data storage.
• Twilio: Provides telecommunications and phone infrastructure.
• Cal.com: Supports appointment booking when used by AetherLabs.
• Make.com: Supports workflow automation and integrations when used by AetherLabs.
• N8N: Supports workflow automation and integrations when used by AetherLabs.

AetherLabs may use additional subprocessors when necessary to deliver its services.

AetherLabs will:

• Maintain an updated list of its subprocessors.
• Notify Clients of changes to its subprocessors at least 30 days in advance. The Client may object on reasonable data protection grounds within 14 days, and the parties will work in good faith to resolve the objection.
• Require subprocessors to protect personal data through appropriate contractual obligations.
• Remain responsible for its obligations concerning the use of subprocessors, as required by applicable law.

Clients may independently choose and connect their own scheduling platforms or other third-party services. Platforms selected and independently managed by the Client are not automatically AetherLabs subprocessors.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Unless otherwise agreed:

• Call recordings and transcripts are retained for 90 days by default.
• Clients may request extended retention for up to 180 days.
• Clients may request deletion of their personal data during the service period.
• Upon termination of the services, AetherLabs will, at the Client's choice, return or delete personal data within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements.
• Deletion from third-party providers is subject to their technical capabilities and applicable retention requirements.

Other information may be retained where necessary to provide the services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

9. Personal Data Breaches

If AetherLabs becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting the Client's personal data, it will:

• Notify the affected Client without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
• Provide available information about the nature of the breach, affected data, and potential consequences.
• Take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and mitigate the breach.
• Cooperate with the Client where reasonably necessary to support its legal reporting obligations.

10. International Data Transfers

Personal data may be processed or stored in the United States and other countries depending on the infrastructure providers and services used.

AetherLabs will take reasonable steps to ensure international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

Where personal data subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, the parties agree that the Standard Contractual Clauses (controller-to-processor module) are incorporated into this DPA by reference. For personal data subject to KVKK, the parties will use the applicable Turkish standard contract or another legally recognized transfer mechanism.

Information about relevant international processing arrangements will be made available to Clients upon request.

11. Client Responsibilities

The Client is responsible for:

• Establishing a valid legal basis for collecting and processing callers' personal data.
• Obtaining any required consent for call recording, automated calls, and telemarketing activities, including compliance with applicable recording consent laws, the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, do-not-call rules, and calling-time restrictions.
• Informing callers that they are speaking with an AI voice agent, where required by law, and configuring the agent's opening disclosure accordingly.
• Providing callers with legally required privacy notices and information.
• Not instructing AetherLabs to collect sensitive information that the services are not designed to handle.
• Ensuring its instructions to AetherLabs comply with applicable laws.
• Providing accurate information and updating it when necessary.
• Handling data subject requests, with reasonable assistance from AetherLabs.

The Client remains responsible for its own legal obligations as Data Controller. Further terms on these responsibilities, including indemnification, are set out in the Terms of Service.

12. Data Subject Requests

AetherLabs will reasonably assist the Client with requests from individuals to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal data.

If AetherLabs receives a relevant request directly from an individual, it will forward the request to the appropriate Client where legally permitted.

AetherLabs will not independently respond to such requests unless legally required or authorized by the Client.

AetherLabs will provide reasonable technical assistance to help the Client fulfill its data protection obligations, including data protection impact assessments and consultations with regulators where required.

13. Audits and Compliance

AetherLabs will make available reasonable information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

Clients may request relevant security and data protection information.

Where reasonably necessary, Clients may conduct audits subject to:

• Reasonable advance written notice.
• Normal business hours.
• Appropriate confidentiality and security requirements.
• Measures to prevent unreasonable disruption to AetherLabs' operations or exposure of other clients' information.

14. Liability

Each party is responsible for its own obligations under applicable data protection laws.

AetherLabs is responsible for its processing activities and contractual obligations as Data Processor.

The liability limitations established in the applicable Terms of Service also apply to this DPA to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

Nothing in this DPA excludes or limits liability where such exclusion or limitation is prohibited by applicable law.

15. Termination and Data Return

This DPA remains effective for as long as AetherLabs processes personal data on behalf of the Client.

Either party may terminate this DPA when the underlying service agreement ends.

Upon termination, AetherLabs will return or delete personal data in accordance with Section 8, subject to applicable legal requirements.

16. Relationship with the Service Agreement

This DPA forms part of the service agreement between AetherLabs and the Client.

If there is a conflict between this DPA and the service agreement concerning personal data processing, this DPA will take precedence for that subject matter.

All other terms of the service agreement remain in effect.

17. Governing Law

This DPA will be governed by the laws specified in the applicable service agreement, subject to mandatory data protection laws that apply to the parties.

18. Agreement and Acceptance

This Data Processing Agreement forms part of the applicable service agreement between AetherLabs and the Client.

By entering into a service agreement with AetherLabs, the Client acknowledges and agrees to the terms of this DPA where AetherLabs processes personal data on the Client's behalf.

This DPA does not require a separate signature unless otherwise agreed by the parties or required by applicable law. A signed copy is available on request at the email below.
