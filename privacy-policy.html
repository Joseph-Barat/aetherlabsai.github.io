Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AetherLabs.

We provide AI-powered voice agent services to businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

This policy applies to our website and services. For information about callers handled by our AI voice agents on behalf of our clients, we act as a service provider (processor); see Section 9.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Business and Client Information

• Business name
• Contact person's name
• Email address
• Phone number
• Business website
• Business location
• Billing and payment information

Caller Information

When our AI voice agents handle calls, they may collect:

• Caller's name and phone number
• Email address
• Call recordings and transcripts
• Appointment details
• Information shared during conversations

Our services are not designed to collect sensitive information (such as health information, government ID numbers, or payment card numbers) unless a client has separately agreed this with us in writing and the law permits it.

Website Booking Information

When you book a consultation through Cal.com, we collect your business name, contact person's name, email address, website, and business location.

Website and Usage Information

• IP address, browser type, and device information
• Pages viewed, links clicked, and time spent on our website
• Product usage events within our dashboard
• Information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 8)

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

• Provide and operate our AI voice agent services.
• Handle calls and appointment bookings.
• Manage client accounts and payments.
• Provide call recordings, transcripts, and reports.
• Improve our website and product, using website and usage data.
• Respond to requests and provide support.

We do not use client or caller information for advertising, marketing, or AI model training without separate written permission.

4. Call Recordings and Data Retention

Call recordings and transcripts are normally retained for 90 days.

If a client requests extended retention, the data may be kept for up to 180 days maximum.

After the applicable retention period, recordings and transcripts are deleted from our systems. Deletion from third-party providers depends on their technical capabilities.

Account and billing information is kept for the life of the account and for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. Other information may be retained where necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Providers

We use third-party providers to operate our services. These may include:

• Retell AI: AI voice agents and call processing.
• Twilio: Phone numbers and telephony.
• Cal.com: Appointment scheduling.
• Make.com and n8n: Automations and integrations.
• Google Analytics: Website analytics.
• Mixpanel: Product and usage analytics.
• Whop and Stripe: Payments and billing.

These providers may process information on our behalf to deliver their services.

Google Analytics and Mixpanel are used for website and product analytics only and do not receive call recordings, transcripts, or caller information. [CONFIRM THIS IS TRUE]

We do not store full payment card numbers. Payments are handled by Stripe and Whop. [CONFIRM THIS IS TRUE]

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Data Storage and Security

The United States is our primary data storage location.

Some third-party providers may process or access information in other locations, depending on their infrastructure.

We rely on security measures provided by our service providers, including encryption. Access to customer information is restricted to authorized personnel, and we use two-factor authentication on our accounts. More detail is available on our Security page.

However, no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data Access and Deletion

Clients can access their call recordings and transcripts through our dashboard.

Clients may also request the deletion of customer information handled through our services.

Deletion requests can be submitted to:

Contact@aetherlabsai.com

We will review and process requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our service agreements.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

We use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to understand website and service usage, measure performance, and improve user experience.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

• Request access to your personal information.
• Request correction of inaccurate information.
• Request deletion of your information.
• Object to or restrict certain processing.
• Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For information about callers, AetherLabs acts as a processor on behalf of the client that operates the AI voice agent. Callers should contact the business they called. If we receive a request about a call handled for a client, we will forward it to that client where the law allows.

For all other requests (for example, from website visitors, prospective clients, and client account holders), contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our response or emailing us with the subject "Appeal."

10. AI Voice Agents and Recording

Our voice agents are AI systems. Our clients are responsible for telling callers they are speaking with an AI, for recording notices, and for obtaining any consent required by law. We do not create voiceprints. See our AI & Recording Policy for details.

11. State and International Rights

California residents: if the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to our processing of your information, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose, to request deletion and correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request.

EU, UK, and Türkiye: if the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) applies, you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not designed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. For material changes, we will also notify our clients by email.
