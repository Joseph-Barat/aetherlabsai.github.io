AI & Call Recording Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

AetherLabs provides AI-powered voice agents and automated communication services to businesses.

This policy explains how our AI voice agents operate, how calls may be recorded and transcribed, how AI-generated information is handled, and the responsibilities of businesses using our services.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Data Processing Agreement.

2. AI Disclosure and Transparency

Our AI voice agents are designed to communicate naturally with callers, but not to deceive people about what they are.

• AetherLabs does not support turning off the AI disclosure on outbound calls or where applicable law requires it.
• If a caller asks whether they are speaking with an AI, the agent must answer truthfully and identify itself as an AI assistant.
• AI agents must not intentionally misrepresent themselves as human representatives.
• Clients are responsible for ensuring that any legally required AI disclosures are provided when using our services. Depending on the call, these may come from federal rules, state laws, or, for calls reaching people in the European Union, the EU AI Act.

3. Call Recording and Transcription

Calls handled through AetherLabs services may be recorded and transcribed depending on the client's configuration.

Recordings and transcripts may be used for:

• Providing and operating AI voice agent services.
• Generating call summaries and transcripts.
• Reviewing call outcomes and interactions.
• Supporting service monitoring and troubleshooting.

Where recording is enabled, the agent can state a recording notice at the start of the call, and clients can configure its wording.

Clients are responsible for obtaining any legally required consent and providing required notices before recording calls. Some US states require every party on a call to consent to recording, so clients should use a recording notice for any call that may involve a person in one of those states.

AetherLabs personnel access recordings and transcripts only to provide support, troubleshoot issues, maintain the services, or as the client instructs.

Call recordings and transcripts are retained for 90 days by default. Clients may request extended retention for up to 180 days.

Clients may also request the deletion of their recordings and transcripts, subject to applicable legal requirements and third-party provider capabilities. More detail is in our Data Processing Agreement.

4. AI-Generated Content and Accuracy

AetherLabs uses AI technology to generate responses, summaries, transcripts, and other information during calls.

Although we aim to provide reliable services:

• AI-generated responses may occasionally contain inaccurate or incomplete information.
• Automatically generated call summaries and transcripts may contain errors.
• AI agents should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional legal, medical, or financial advice.
• The services are not designed for emergency calls, and clients should configure their agents to direct callers to emergency services where relevant.
• Clients are responsible for reviewing important AI-generated information before relying on it for significant business decisions.

5. AI Data Usage and Training

AetherLabs does not use client or caller data for its own AI model training, advertising, or marketing without separate written permission.

AetherLabs does not create voiceprints or use voice recordings to identify or verify individuals.

Third-party technology providers process information under their own privacy policies and contractual terms. Where our providers offer settings that limit the use of data for model training or service improvement, we use those settings.

Clients may contact AetherLabs for further information about how personal data is processed by our technology providers.

6. Human Intervention and Call Transfers

AetherLabs AI voice agents may transfer calls to human representatives when this functionality is enabled by the client.

• Call transfers depend on the client's phone system and routing configuration.
• Successful transfers depend on human representative availability.
• If a representative is unavailable, the AI agent may offer an alternative, such as taking a message or arranging a callback.
• AetherLabs does not guarantee that a human representative will always be available.

Clients decide whether to enable human call transfers or operate their AI voice agents without human intervention.

7. AI Limitations and Client Responsibilities

AI voice agents are automated systems and may have limitations.

• AI agents may misunderstand callers, provide inaccurate responses, or fail to recognize certain requests.
• AI agents may experience interruptions or service limitations caused by third-party providers.
• Clients are responsible for reviewing their AI agent's configuration, instructions, and business information.

Clients are responsible for determining whether the services are appropriate for their business operations.

8. Third-Party Technology Providers

AetherLabs relies on third-party infrastructure and technology providers to deliver its services.

These may include:

• Retell AI: AI voice agent infrastructure, call processing, and data storage.
• Twilio: Telecommunications and phone infrastructure.
• Other providers: Additional platforms used for integrations, scheduling, automation, and related services. The full list is in our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

These providers may process call-related information to deliver their services.

Their own privacy policies, security practices, and contractual terms may also apply.

Third-party providers may experience outages, interruptions, or technical limitations that affect service availability. AetherLabs remains responsible for its own contractual and legal obligations.

9. Outbound Calls and Opt-Outs

Clients who use our AI voice agents to place outbound calls are responsible for complying with all laws that apply to those calls, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), do-not-call rules, and state telemarketing laws.

• Consent: the client must have the consent required by law before each call. Calls that use AI-generated voices generally require the same consent as calls that use pre-recorded or artificial voices, and marketing calls generally require prior express written consent.
• Do-not-call lists: the client must check numbers against applicable national and state do-not-call lists and its own internal list.
• Calling hours: the client must follow applicable calling-time restrictions for the recipient's location.
• Caller ID: calls must display accurate caller identification.
• Opt-outs: if someone asks not to be called again, the agent will end the call politely, and the client is responsible for removing that number from future calls promptly.
• Disclosure: outbound agents identify themselves as AI at the start of the call (see Section 2).

AetherLabs may ask clients for evidence of consent, and may pause, refuse, or suspend any outbound campaign that it reasonably believes does not comply with applicable law or our Terms of Service.

10. Sensitive Information and Prohibited Uses

Our services are not designed to collect sensitive information such as health information, government ID numbers, or payment card numbers unless separately agreed in writing. Clients should not configure agents to ask callers for this information and should direct callers to a secure channel instead.

Clients may not use our services to impersonate people or organizations, clone a real person's voice without that person's consent, deceive or harass people, or make calls without the legal right to do so. The full Acceptable Use rules are in our Terms of Service.

11. Policy Updates

AetherLabs may update this policy as its services, technology, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on the AetherLabs website with a revised effective or last-updated date. For material changes, we will also notify clients by email.

Clients are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
