Security

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Protecting Business and Customer Data

At AetherLabs, protecting client information and maintaining the security of our AI voice agent infrastructure are important priorities.

We use established technology providers and security measures to help protect the information processed through our services. Our approach focuses on data protection, controlled access, encryption, and responsible data handling.

These services are intended for business use only.

AetherLabs is currently a business brand operated by an individual, and is not a separate incorporated legal entity. The individual operating the service is the contracting service provider.

2. Data Protection and Encryption

Client and caller information is processed through the technology infrastructure used to deliver our services, including Retell AI and Twilio.

Our infrastructure providers maintain security measures designed to protect information, including encryption, access restrictions, and other technical safeguards.

Retell AI's security documentation describes data encryption, restricted production access, and controls for protecting stored information. Twilio also documents encryption for data in transit and at rest across its applicable services.

The specific protections applied may depend on the provider, service, and configuration being used.

Our services are not designed to handle sensitive information such as protected health information or payment card numbers unless separately agreed in writing. See our Data Processing Agreement for details.

3. Infrastructure Security

AetherLabs relies on established third-party infrastructure providers to support the delivery of its AI voice agent services.

Our primary infrastructure providers include:

• Retell AI: Provides our AI voice agent platform and processes and stores voice-related information, including call recordings and transcripts.
• Twilio: Provides telecommunications infrastructure and supports the delivery and routing of voice communications.

We also use other providers for scheduling, workflow automation, analytics, and payments. The full list is in our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

These providers maintain their own security programs, technical safeguards, and compliance documentation. Their certifications and security practices belong to the respective providers and do not represent independent certifications held by AetherLabs.

4. Access Control

Access to client information is restricted to authorized AetherLabs personnel, who are required to keep it confidential.

Clients can also access their own call recordings and transcripts through the available dashboard.

We use access restrictions and two-factor authentication (2FA) to help protect accounts and systems against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Call recordings and transcripts are retained for 90 days by default.

Clients may request extended retention for up to 180 days.

Clients can also request the deletion of their data by contacting AetherLabs. When a client ends their service, we return or delete their data at the client's choice within [30] days.

Data handling and deletion are subject to applicable legal requirements and the capabilities of our service providers.

6. Security Incident Response

AetherLabs takes security incidents seriously.

If a security incident affects client data, we will notify the affected clients without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We assess and handle incidents as they arise and take appropriate steps to address identified issues.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please email us at the address below with the subject "Security." We will review reports and respond as soon as reasonably possible.

7. Security Standards and Certifications

AetherLabs currently does not hold independent SOC 2 (Type I or Type II) or ISO 27001 certification.

Our services rely on the security infrastructure and practices of our third-party technology providers. We aim to maintain responsible security practices as our services and operations develop.
