Frequently
asked questions

Why should we choose you, over others?
Choose us if we can show the value before you start. We show you what your missed calls cost you, today and over time, then show a demo so you hear it work first. If we don’t see a clear return, we’ll tell you.
What makes you different from others?
What makes us different isvoice quality , security , and transparency . Our agent has a natural, human-sounding voice. Your calls and customer data stay secure and private. And your dashboard shows every call with audio, transcript, and summary.
What’s your pricing?
We price based on the value the agent creates, not our hours. If you miss 30 calls a month at $200 each, that’s $6,000 walking away, and recovering even half pays for itself many times over. Pricing is a one-time setup fee plus a monthly fee covering hosting, calls, dashboard, and optimization. After the call, we give you a fixed quote with no hidden fees.
Will it sound robotic or annoy customers?
You hear a demo before you commit. We script conversations carefully, tune the voice and pacing, and test with real calls before going live. Your dashboard lets you listen to any call and read the transcript anytime. If a caller is frustrated or asks for a person, the agent transfers them to you or anyone responsible.
Do we have to replace our receptionist?
No. We’re not here to replace your team. The agent covers what your receptionist can’t: after-hours calls, busy periods, and missed calls. It handles 10 calls at the same time, so no caller gets a busy signal or waits on hold. Your receptionist stays focused on the people in front of them, and you won’t need extra headcount to cover more calls.
How do we track performance and see results?
Every call appears in your dashboard with the time, a short summary, the full audio, and the transcript. No waiting for a report. Most clients go live within 1-2 weeks, and you’ll see results in the first week after launch, based on call volume.
What if we’re unhappy or want to cancel?
You can cancel anytime, no long-term lock-in. If the agent isn’t delivering, we fix it, and you can see the results in your dashboard anytime.
